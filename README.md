# SaaS Technology Performance Analysis — 2024

**Analyst:** Senior Data Analyst  
**Contact:** 24f2007947@ds.study.iitm.ac.in

## Objective
Investigate slowing Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth and recommend actions to reach the industry benchmark.

## Dataset (2024 Quarterly MRR Growth)
- Q1: 4.99
- Q2: 5.34
- Q3: 10.62
- Q4: 13.75

**Average (required): 8.68**  
**Industry Target:** 15

## Key Findings
1. 2024 average MRR growth is **8.68%**, significantly below the **15%** benchmark.
2. Growth accelerated in H2 (Q3–Q4) but still fell short of target in Q4 (**13.75%**).
3. The gap to target narrowed from **~10pp in H1** to **~1–6pp in H2**, indicating positive momentum that can be amplified with focused investments.

## Business Implications
- Current growth trajectory risks underperforming FY targets and delaying roadmap investments.
- Without action, the compounding gap vs. peers will widen, pressuring CAC payback and net retention.

## Recommendations
- **Expand into new market segments** (core directive): Prioritize 2–3 high-propensity verticals (e.g., FinTech, Healthcare SaaS) where feature fit is strong.
- **Segmented packaging & pricing:** Introduce vertical add-ons and usage-based tiers to capture willingness-to-pay.
- **Demand-gen reallocation:** Shift 20–30% of paid budget toward channels proven for the target segments; double down on partner-led motion.
- **Activation & Expansion:** Launch vertical onboarding playbooks; align CSMs with industry expertise to drive expansion and reduce time-to-value.
